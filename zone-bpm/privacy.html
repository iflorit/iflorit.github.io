Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Your data stays yours

ZoneBPM reads your heart rate (BPM) through Apple HealthKit solely to display your training zones in real time. This health data never leaves your device or your personal iCloud account.

What we don't collect

We never send your personal or health data to our servers or third parties. We use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics strictly for anonymous, aggregated usage and crash data to help improve app stability.

  • No sale or advertising use of health data.
  • No personal health profiles stored on ZoneBPM servers.
  • No sharing of your workout data with third parties.

Calendar & EventKit usage

ZoneBPM requests write-only access to your calendar. This is used exclusively to save completed workouts to your schedule. We cannot and do not read your existing calendar events.

Your right to delete

You are always in control. Because your health data lives on your device, you can delete your workout data directly from the Apple Health app at any time.

Questions
